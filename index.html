First time?

Here's how to enjoy it

  1. Order

    Scan the QR menu (it can be translated into your language) to order, or ask our staff for a paper menu.

  2. No grilling for you to do

    Enjoy the complimentary beef soup that comes first. While you do, your food arrives fully grilled.

  3. Mix in the vegetables

    Everything comes 100% grilled. Mix the bean sprouts and chives served on top of the gopchang with the green onion kimchi. It tastes even better.

  4. Turn the burner off

    If the burner stays on, the gopchang burns and loses its flavor. (Turn it OFF after mixing in the vegetables.)
